## 4. Policy proposal vs. the "never weaken GAP-026" rule — FLAGGED

Repo rule, verbatim (`AGENTS.md:49`): **"GAP-026 cannot be 'fixed' by raising tolerance."**

`GAP026_POLICY_PROPOSAL.md` status line says `DRAFT — FOR INDEPENDENT REVIEW ONLY` and
explicitly states in §9 Non-Goals: *"No change to the current pin's tolerance (0.25 m /
12°) in this proposal"* and *"No wiring of a hard gate before independent review."*
Confirmed by `grep`: nothing in `ultimate_pipeline/quality/*.py` or
`scripts/measure_candidate_acceptance.py` currently consumes `junction_lanelink_sanity`
— so as of this commit, **nothing is actually wired, and the checker's own algorithmic
tolerance (0.25 m / 12°) is genuinely untouched.**

**However**, the proposal's substantive content (§3–§6), if it were later adopted as
written, does something that functions as tolerance-raising even though it is careful
not to call itself that:

- It defines a *new, separate, looser* "hard-fail" gating threshold at **0.50 m**
  (double the checker's native 0.25 m pass line), and explicitly places the
  0.25–0.50 m band — **1,721 of 2,992 failures, 57.5% of the total failure
  population** — into a "warning"/"WAIVED-eligible" bucket that is **not** required
  to block promotion, only to be documented with a waiver record.
- The example waiver text in §6 is itself illustrative of this: it proposes waiving
  exactly this population with `expiration_or_review_trigger: "re-review when stage_07
  lane_width harmonization lands"` — i.e., open-ended until someone gets around to the
  actual fix.
- §6 also opens a path to waive part of the **severe (>3 m) tail** itself (the 704
  "outer→inner lane-count mismatch" failures) via a "bounded outer-lane isolation
  waiver," provided every affected link is enumerated — a materially better-designed
  waiver (exhaustive, per-link, expiring) than a blanket suppression, but it is still a
  mechanism for **known, currently-failing, driving-traversable laneLinks to be
  excluded from a hard block** rather than required to be fixed before promotion.

**Net assessment:** the proposal does not alter the checker's own 0.25 m/12° pass/fail
line, and nothing is wired yet — so it does not currently violate the letter of
AGENTS.md:49. But if adopted as written, it would let roughly 57.5% of the checker's
own known failures (plus a defined subset of the severe tail) through a promotion gate
as "waived" rather than blocking, which is functionally equivalent to raising the
*effective* (gating) tolerance from 0.25 m to 0.50 m for the majority of the
population. This is exactly the kind of change the rule appears designed to prevent,
and it is the single most important thing for a human reviewer to weigh before
approving this proposal — **this file should not be approved or wired without that
question being explicitly decided**, which is also what the document itself asks for
("FOR INDEPENDENT REVIEW ONLY", "No hard gate may be wired until this proposal is
independently reviewed and approved").
